# Two-plant dispatch

Interactive linear programme for *Optimisation in Energy Systems*, Session 1: choose coal and gas
output to meet demand at the lowest cost.

**Open it:** https://timsigner.github.io/two-plant-dispatch/

- Drag the orange cost line towards cheaper costs. At the last contact with the feasible region it snaps
  to the optimum. Arrow keys also move it once the graph is selected; Home jumps to the optimum.
- Change capacities, costs, demand and the emissions cap on the left; "Reset to lecture defaults"
  restores the lecture values.
- Works best on a laptop or a large screen. To use it offline, download `index.html` and open it in a
  browser: it is a single file and needs no internet.

Emission factors: coal 0.76 and gas 0.37 t CO2/MWh, the median direct emissions in IPCC AR5,
Working Group III, Annex III, Table A.III.2. All other numbers are illustrative teaching values.
